RemoteVanta - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 6, 2026

Welcome to RemoteVanta - Universal TV Remote. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information and device permissions are handled when you use the RemoteVanta mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

RemoteVanta does not require users to create an account, register, or sign in.

We do not operate our own user-data collection server. The app may process information required for its features, including local network information, TV connection details, device information, and app preferences.

Saved TV connections and preferences may be stored locally on your device to make future connections easier.

2. Wi-Fi and Local Network Access

RemoteVanta uses network access to discover, connect to, and communicate with compatible TVs on your local Wi-Fi network.

Depending on the TV and connection method, the app may process local network information such as IP addresses, device names, and connection details.

This information is used to establish and maintain the TV remote-control connection.

3. Microphone and Voice Control

RemoteVanta may request microphone permission when you use the voice typing feature.

The microphone is used to capture your spoken words and convert them into text through speech recognition.

Speech recognition may be performed by an Android system service or another configured third-party speech recognition provider. Depending on the provider and your device settings, audio may be transmitted to that provider for processing. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to that processing.

Recognized text is used as keyboard input in the app. You can choose to send that text to your connected TV.

You can manage or revoke microphone permission through your Android device settings. Voice typing will not work if microphone permission is denied.

4. TV Remote Control Features

RemoteVanta provides remote-control features such as navigation buttons, touchpad gestures, keyboard input, volume controls, and other supported TV commands.

Commands and input are sent to the TV you choose to connect to. The app processes this information to perform the actions requested by you.

5. Advertising - Google AdMob

RemoteVanta uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service provided by Google.

Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and other information required for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and related services.

Depending on your location, applicable requirements, and advertising settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

For more information about how Google handles data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

You can also learn more about Google's advertising technologies and controls here: Google Advertising Policies .

6. Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services to provide certain functionality, including Google AdMob for advertisements and speech recognition services for voice typing.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control their independent data-processing practices.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not operate our own service to collect or sell personal information. However, information may be processed by third-party providers when you use their services, as described in this Privacy Policy.

8. Data Security and Retention

TV connection details and app preferences may be stored locally on your device.

You can remove saved TV connections through the app's device-management features. You may also clear the app's data or uninstall the app through Android settings.

Information handled by third-party providers is subject to their respective retention and security policies.

9. Children's Privacy

RemoteVanta is a general-purpose TV remote-control application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our own servers.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage app permissions, including microphone permission, through your Android device settings.

You can also manage advertising preferences and available advertising controls through your device and Google's settings, where supported.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
