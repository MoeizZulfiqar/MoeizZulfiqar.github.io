Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ThermoPrint ("the App"), developed by Moeiz ("we", "us", or "the developer"), handles information when you use the application. By using ThermoPrint, you acknowledge this policy.

1. About ThermoPrint

ThermoPrint is a printing utility designed to help users prepare text, images, and supported documents for printing with compatible printers. Available features may vary by app version and device.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, you may select or enter text, images, documents, and other content for printing. This content may contain personal or business information. Please check your content before printing or sharing it.

We do not intentionally request personal information such as your name, address, or phone number unless a particular feature explicitly requires it. Actual data handling may also depend on third-party services integrated into the App.

3. Files and Device Storage

The App may access files or images that you select or authorize for a feature. Depending on the version and features enabled, content may be stored locally for tasks such as saving templates, preparing documents, or maintaining print history.

You can use available in-app deletion options and your device's storage settings to manage saved content. Removing the App may not delete files that you previously exported or saved separately.

4. Printer and Network Connections

If you print through a network or another supported printer connection, the App processes the information needed to carry out your print request. Network printing may transmit print content over your local network to the selected printer.

Use trusted networks and compatible printers. The privacy and security of data transmitted to a printer may also depend on your network, printer, and device configuration.

This information may be used for delivering advertisements, personalizing ads where permitted, measuring advertising performance, analytics, and detecting invalid activity or fraud.

The information collected and the purposes of processing depend on the AdMob implementation, your consent choices, and your device and privacy settings. We do not claim that all advertisements are personalized or that all data categories listed above are collected in every situation.
