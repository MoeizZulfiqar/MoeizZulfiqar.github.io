Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DocuPDF ("the App"), developed by Moeiz Zulfiqar ("we", "us", or "the developer"), handles information when you use the App. DocuPDF helps you scan, view, organize, and work with PDF files and images.

1. Files, images, and document processing

When you choose a file or image, or scan a paper document, DocuPDF uses that content to provide the feature you requested, such as viewing, converting, organizing, or editing a document. Files that you create or save in the App are stored on your device or in a location you choose. DocuPDF does not operate a server to upload your documents or images. If you choose to share or export a file, it is sent to the app or destination you select through Android's sharing and file-access features.

DocuPDF uses Google's document-scanning component, provided through Google Play services, for its document scanning flow. Google Play services may download or update components needed for that feature. The scanner is subject to Google's terms and privacy information as well as your device configuration.

The OCR feature uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition through Google Play services to process the image or PDF page you select and return recognized text. The recognition model runs on the device; Google Play services may download the model when needed. The recognized text is shown in the App and is not sent by DocuPDF to a developer server.

2. Camera, file access, and permissions

The camera is used when you choose a camera-based scanning feature. You select documents and images using Android's system file picker or the document scanner. Android grants the App access to the content you select. Permission availability and behavior can vary by Android version and device. You can manage permissions in Android settings; denying a permission or access request can prevent the related feature from working.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

DocuPDF uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares certain information to deliver and measure ads, analyze performance, and help prevent fraud. Depending on your device, settings, consent choices, and the ad request, this may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location.
  • Advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account identifiers, where available.
  • Interactions with the App and ads, such as launches, taps, impressions, and video views.
  • Diagnostics and performance information about the App or advertising SDK.

Google and its advertising partners may use information as described in their own policies and the settings that apply to your ad request. We do not control Google's independent data practices. You can review or reset your advertising ID in Android settings where that option is available. Ad availability and personalization depend on your region, settings, and applicable consent requirements.

4. Google Play purchases

DocuPDF offers optional subscriptions and in-app purchases through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the transaction and manages payment details. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. The App receives purchase information from Google Play to provide and restore the premium features you buy. Google Play's handling of payment and account information is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.

5. Local storage, retention, and deletion

Documents and app preferences may remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Files exported or saved to another location must be managed separately in that location. Information held by Google services is retained and managed under Google's applicable policies and settings.

6. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your documents or images. Information may be processed by Google services when needed for advertising, OCR, document scanning, or Google Play purchases. If you choose to share a document, the receiving app or service handles it under its own terms and privacy policy. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and the App.

7. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect the App and its locally stored data. No device, software, or data transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Protect your device and review the destination before sharing a document.

8. Children's privacy

DocuPDF is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and other third-party services may apply additional age-related requirements and settings.

9. Your choices

You can choose which files to open, whether to use the camera scanner, whether to share a document, and whether to make an optional purchase. You can also manage app permissions and available advertising ID settings in Android. Consent options shown to you may depend on your location and applicable requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features, service providers, or data practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or DocuPDF's data practices, contact: moeizzulfiqar@gmail.com.
